# Jalilgym privacy policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Jalilgym is made by CH.Djalil. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. In short: your data stays on your device.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- workouts, sets, programs and personal records
- body weight, measurements and progress photos
- your food diary, custom foods, saved meals and nutrition targets
- the answers you give during setup (goal, experience, sex, birth date, height,
  activity level) and your settings

Jalilgym has no account system and no server of its own. We never receive, see
or sell this information.

## What leaves your device

When you search for a food or look up a barcode, the search words or the barcode
number are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to
[Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a non-profit food database,
to find matching products. The request identifies the app ("Jalilgym") but
contains nothing about you: no name, account, location, advertising ID or
device identifier. Open Food Facts handles these requests under its own
[privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy).

Nothing else is sent anywhere. Jalilgym contains no advertising, analytics or
crash-reporting libraries.

## Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes, on the device, when you open the
scanner. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere. Progress photos you choose
are copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded.

## Notifications

During a workout the app shows a notification with the rest timer. It is created
on the device and contains no data from outside the app.

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on, your app data may be included in your Google
backup, which Google encrypts. Progress photos are always left out of backups.

## Deleting your data

Delete individual entries in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's
storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

## Children

Jalilgym is not directed at children under 13.
