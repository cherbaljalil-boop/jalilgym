# Jalilgym privacy policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Jalilgym is made by CH.Djalil. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. In short: your data stays on your device.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

- workouts, sets, programs and personal records
- body weight, measurements and progress photos
- your food diary, custom foods, saved meals and nutrition targets
- the answers you give during setup (goal, experience, sex, birth date, height,
  activity level) and your settings

Unless you turn on cloud sync (below), Jalilgym has no account and no server.
Nothing you enter is sent anywhere, and we never receive, see or sell it.

## What leaves your device

When you search for a food or look up a barcode, the search words or the barcode
number are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to
[Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a non-profit food database,
to find matching products. The request identifies the app ("Jalilgym") but
contains nothing about you: no name, account, location, advertising ID or
device identifier. Open Food Facts handles these requests under its own
[privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy).

Nothing else is sent anywhere. Jalilgym contains no advertising, analytics or
crash-reporting libraries.

## Cloud sync (optional)

You can create an account in Profile → Cloud sync to keep your data in sync
between phones. If you do:

- **What is collected:** your email address, used to sign you in with a
  one-time code, and a copy of the app's data: workouts, programs, body weight
  and measurements, food diary, foods, recipes, water and grocery list, and
  achievements, plus your setup answers and settings (goal, units, sex, birth
  date, height, activity level, water goal and reminder times) so a new phone
  is set up for you. Progress photos are never uploaded.
- **Where it goes:** a database we run on [Supabase](https://supabase.com),
  sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored encrypted at rest. Each
  account can only read its own data.
- **What it's used for:** only to sync your data to your other devices. It is
  not used for advertising or analytics, and not shared or sold.
- **Deleting it:** Profile → Cloud sync → Delete account removes your account
  and everything synced to it from the server straight away. Signing out keeps
  the account and stops syncing on that phone. Data on your phone is not
  deleted either way.

## Health Connect (optional)

If you turn on Health Connect in Profile and grant access, Jalilgym exchanges
data with Health Connect, Android's on-device health data store:

- **Written:** your finished workouts (as strength-training sessions with their
  start and end times), the body weights you log, and the calories and
  nutrients of each meal in your diary, for the last 30 days.
- **Read:** body weights recorded by other apps (for example a smart scale),
  which are added to your weight history, and today's step count, which is
  shown on the Today screen.

This data moves only between Jalilgym and Health Connect on your device.
Jalilgym never sends it to us or to anyone else, and doesn't use it for
advertising. Jalilgym's use of data from Health Connect follows the
[Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134),
including its Limited Use requirements. You can turn the integration off in
Profile, and remove Jalilgym's access or its data in Health Connect settings,
at any time.

## Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes, on the device, when you open the
scanner. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere. Progress photos you choose
are copied into the app's private storage and never uploaded.

## Notifications

During a workout the app shows a notification with the rest timer. If you turn
them on in Profile, the app also shows workout reminders and a weekly summary.
All notifications are created on the device from your own data, and nothing is
sent anywhere to produce them.

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on, your app data may be included in your Google
backup, which Google encrypts. Progress photos are always left out of backups.

## Deleting your data

If you use cloud sync, delete your account in Profile → Cloud sync to remove
your data from the server. Delete individual entries in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's
storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

## Children

Jalilgym is not directed at children under 13.
