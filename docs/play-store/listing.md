## Full description (4,000)

Jalilgym is a training and nutrition companion for people who lift. Pick a proven program or build your own, log every set in seconds, and eat for your goal, all on your phone, with no account and no ads.

TRAIN WITH STRUCTURE
• Push/Pull/Legs (3 or 6 days) and Full Body programs, or a custom program builder with supersets
• Automatic progression: when you hit the top of your rep range, next time's weight goes up
• Fast logging with last session's numbers pre-filled, warm-up, drop and failure sets, and RPE or RIR
• Rest timer that keeps running on the lock screen, with notification controls
• Plate calculator, 1RM estimator and warm-up generator
• About 100 exercises with photos, instructions and a muscle map, plus your own custom exercises

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Personal records detected automatically, with history
• Strength charts for estimated 1RM, top weight and volume
• Weekly muscle-volume heatmap
• Body weight with a 7-day trend, body measurements and private progress photos with a before/after slider
• Training streaks that rest days never break, and 24 achievements to unlock

EAT FOR YOUR GOAL
• Calorie and macro targets for cutting, lean bulking or maintenance, with an optional high-protein style
• Food diary with a calorie ring, macro bars, fiber, sugar, saturated fat and sodium
• Search millions of products from Open Food Facts, or scan a barcode
• Halal filter: products labeled halal are marked, and pork, gelatin and alcohol are flagged
• Custom foods, quick add, saved meals and "copy yesterday's breakfast"
• Common foods and everything you've used before work offline

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Only food searches and barcode lookups go to Open Food Facts, with nothing that identifies you.
• Progress photos never leave your phone and are left out of backups.

Calorie and macro targets are estimates and not medical advice.

Created by CH.Djalil.
